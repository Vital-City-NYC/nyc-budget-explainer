## C. Gaps

1. **OMB's own June 2026 Adopted Financial Plan and June 2025 Adopted Plan documents were not located.** Guessed URLs (`finplan06-26`, `mm6-26`, `sum6-26`) returned nyc.gov 404 pages, and the OMB publications page renders in JavaScript. Use the Comptroller tables, which cite OMB, or find the OMB links by hand. A search also turned up a candidate FY2026 file, https://www.nyc.gov/assets/omb/downloads/pdf/adopt26/adopt26-expreso.pdf (not fetched).
2. FY2027 class tax rates (adopted June 2026) were not found. The DOF FY2027 annual report is not out yet.
3. **CBC effective tax rates by property type:** the cbcny.org pages sit behind a Cloudflare bot check, so I could not read them. Read them by hand in a normal browser.
4. Pied-à-terre surcharge: rate tiers, whether the thresholds are market or assessed value, and the chapter number are unconfirmed. Read bill A.10009-C / S.9009-C (Part ?) on nysenate.gov.
5. Whether NYC tax extender S10609 / A11535 was signed (shown as "Delivered to Governor" when fetched).
6. Legal basis for the UBT rate and the hotel tax rate (does raising them need Albany?), and whether the Council can *raise* the commercial rent tax alone.
7. Whether the UBT-credit cut ($68M) was enacted, and by which body.
8. The signing date of the state budget (May 28, 2026) comes from search summaries only. The budget.ny.gov release reset the connection twice.
9. Share of New York City households that rent (for the renter-burden section). Not sourced this session.
