## Gaps, basis changes and open questions

**Gaps**
- Class 2 split, FY2000-FY2004: no share recorded (null). Those editions have no levy-by-property-type table. They show Class 2 property types only by assessed value ("Taxable Assessment Profile, Citywide"). For reference only, not used: the rental share of Class 2 billable assessed value (Rentals + 4-10 Fam. Rentals over the Class 2 total) was 55.59% (FY2000, src/fy00.pdf PDF p. 13), 55.96% (FY2001, src/fy01.pdf p. 11), 55.93% (FY2002, src/fy02.pdf p. 13), 55.82% (FY2003, src/fy03.pdf p. 18) and 55.99% (FY2004, src/fy04.pdf p. 10). In FY2005-FY2026, where both measures exist, the assessed-value share runs 0.1 to 0.6 points above the levy share: the levy roll adds back STAR value, and that sits mostly in co-ops and condos. If those AV shares were used for FY2000-FY2004 they would likely overstate the rental share by about half a point. Not used, because they are not levy figures.
- No other gaps in the four-class series. All 27 years pass the 0.1 check (largest gap 0.1, in FY2000, 2003, 2004, 2012-14, 2017-18, 2021-22, 2026).

**Basis changes**
- Conrentals (condo buildings containing rental units) first appear as a separate Class 2 row in the FY2010 edition. The FY2010 edition says that in the FY09 report "these parcels were classified as residential condos." So in FY2005-FY2009 those buildings are counted as owner units, which pushes the rental share down a little in those years. The size is small at the break: Conrentals levy was 23.0 of 6,589.8 in FY2010 (0.35% of Class 2). By FY2026 it was 381.2 (2.6%).
- Also in FY2010, Class 1 "Other" took in some parcels the FY09 edition counted as one- and two-family homes (FY2010 edition footnote). That is within Class 1 and does not affect class totals or the Class 2 split.
- The gross levy column is called "Tax Levy (Sch. Tax)" in FY2005-FY2018 and sits after "Tax Excl. Vets" and "Veterans". From FY2019 it is called "Tax Levy" and there is no veterans column. In every edition the Class 2 row in that column matches (or is within 0.5 of) the history-table Class 2 levy, so it is the same gross-levy basis as FY2026.
- FY2003 and FY2009 had midyear rate changes (Table 20 in later editions lists "1st half"/"2nd half" rates). The history tables give a single full-year levy for each, which is what is used.
- FY2000-FY2004 levies come from four different editions (FY2019-FY2023 history tables). Every edition that prints those years shows the same figures, so there is no basis break.

**Figures that differ between editions, or that I'm unsure of**
- FY2011: the FY2026 (and FY2024, FY2025) history table gives total 18,323.7 and Class 4 7,308.0. The FY2011-FY2023 editions all give 18,323.8 and 7,308.1. I used the FY2026 figures, as instructed.
- FY2012: the FY2012 edition gives total 19,284.6 and Class 4 7,670.5. FY2026 gives 19,284.5 and 7,670.4. I used FY2026.
- FY2006: the FY2006 edition's own history row differs from the FY2023 history row (Class 1 +0.5, Class 2 -0.5, Class 4 -0.1). Its property-type table puts Class 2 at 4,842.0, against 4,842.5 in the history table. I used the history table, and the share is applied to 4,842.5.
- FY2007: the property-type table puts Class 2 at 5,217.6, against 5,217.8 in the history table. Rounding-scale only.
- FY2016 Rentals row (src/fy16.pdf p. 36): Tax Excl. Vets 3,798.3, Veterans 0.0, Tax Levy 3,798.7. The row is internally off by 0.4 as printed. I used the printed Tax Levy figure, 3,798.7. It moves the FY2016 share by under 0.01 point.
- In some years the property-type rows add to the Class 2 row only within 0.1-0.2 (rounding in the source). The share uses the printed Class 2 row as its denominator.
- Text was taken from the PDF text layer, not by OCR. The FY2025 and FY2026 results match the expected values exactly: rentals 7,401.6 and 7,628.1, owner units 7,027.3 and 7,241.2. Class totals in each property-type table match the history tables. Together those confirm the column alignment for both table layouts.
