## Verification pass, 2026-10-02 (second session)

Items above marked "via WebFetch" or UNVERIFIED were re-checked against primary pages. Results:

1. **NY Focus, May 29 2026: CONFIRMED verbatim.** "The budget does not hike personal income taxes or corporate taxes, despite a push by New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and both legislative chambers." and "The budget does not give New York City permission to raise its personal income or corporate tax rates — an idea that Mayor Zohran Mamdani floated after campaigning on statewide hikes." Also: "It does extend a pandemic-era corporate tax hike by three years — maintaining the current 7.25 percent rate through 2029." https://nysfocus.com/2026/05/29/new-york-final-state-budget-2026-funding-guide-hochul
2. **NY Focus, Mar 10 2026: CONFIRMED.** "both houses signed onto Mamdani's request to raise New York City's own corporate tax rates." https://nysfocus.com/2026/03/10/senate-assembly-mamdani-tax-hikes
3. **City tax extender: CORRECTION.** S10609 passed the Senate on June 2, 2026 and was "DELIVERED TO ASSEMBLY", not to the governor (https://assembly.ny.gov/leg/?bn=S10609&term=2025). The same text was enacted as Part D of A.11561/S.10644, **Chapter 127 of 2026, signed June 5, 2026** (Assembly actions: "06/05/2026 delivered to governor 06/05/2026 signed chap.127", https://assembly.ny.gov/leg/?bn=A11561&term=2025). Part D text (https://assembly.state.ny.us/leg/?bn=A11561&term=2025&Text=Y): PIT, Admin Code 11-1701 amended so the current rates apply "for taxable years beginning before two thousand [twenty-seven] thirty"; GCT, Admin Code 11-604 rates apply "before January first, two thousand [twenty-seven] thirty"; and chapter 884 of 1975 "shall expire on December 31, [2026] 2029". So the city's PIT and corporation tax rates now run through 2029 and lapse unless Albany acts again.
4. **State budget signing date: CONFIRMED May 28, 2026.** Governor's release, "Governor Kathy Hochul today signed her FY 2027 Budget making New York more affordable, keeping New Yorkers safe and expanding opportunity for all." https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-signs-budget-makes-new-york-more-affordable-keeps-new-yorkers-safe-and-expands. Assembly actions for S9009-C: "05/28/2026 SIGNED CHAP.59".
5. **Second-home surcharge: CONFIRMED from bill text.** S.9009-C / A.10009-C, Part HH (Chapter 59 of 2026), adding Tax Law Article 30-C. https://assembly.state.ny.us/leg/?bn=S09009&term=2025&Text=Y. Thresholds are Department of Finance market value, not assessed value: in phase one (fiscal 2027 and 2028) class one homes at "five million dollars" and condo or co-op units at "one million dollars" of DOF's current market value; from July 1, 2028, everything at $5 million of a comparable-sales value "without regard to the restrictions described in section five hundred eighty-one of the real property tax law". Rates, section 1353: class one 0.8 / 1.05 / 1.3 percent by tier; condos and co-ops in phase one 4.0 / 5.25 / 6.5 percent. Sunset: "shall expire and be deemed repealed on June 30, 2031." The governor's release: "a surcharge that will apply to high-value – $5 million or more – second homes within New York City" ... "expected to generate at least $500 million a year". Mayor's Office Apr 15 release: "It is projected to generate $500 million in annual revenue."
6. **Hotel tax: CONFIRMED extended by local law.** Local Law 153 of 2023 (Int. 1208), "passed by the Council on November 2, 2023 and approved by the Mayor on November 17, 2023", amends Admin Code 11-2502 to keep the rate at "five and seven-eighths percent ... before December first, two thousand [twenty-three] twenty-seven" and "five percent ... on and after December first, two thousand [twenty-three] twenty-seven". https://intro.nyc/local-laws/2023-153. DOF TY2021 report: "raised to the current 5.875 percent in 2009" ... "In 2015, local legislation adopted by the City Council extended the 5.875 percent rate through November 30, 2019." https://www.nyc.gov/assets/finance/downloads/pdf/reports/reports_htx/2021_htx_report.pdf. The underlying state enabling statute was still not located.
7. **UBT rate is state law: CONFIRMED.** General City Model (chapter 772 of the laws of 1966) section 101: "A tax at the rate of four percent is hereby imposed for each taxable year ... on the unincorporated business taxable income of every unincorporated business wholly or partly carried on within the city." https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GCM/101. The mayor's 2026 ask to raise it was carried as a state bill, S9189-A: "AN ACT to amend chapter 772 of the laws of 1966 ... in relation to increasing tax rates imposed on unincorporated businesses and corporations in New York city ... upon adoption of a local law by the local legislative body of the city of New York" (https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/bills/2025/S9189A); still in committee.
8. UBT credit cut ($68M): secondary report only (Hoodline, Jul 17 2026: Preconsidered Intro 972 passed the Council July 16 by 56-8). Not on the page.

Blind-check note (2026-10-02): Chapter 127 of 2026 Part D extends to 2029 the PIT rates, the sales tax on services and the residual general corporation tax rate (Admin Code 11-604, S corporations); it does not touch the business corporation tax (11-654) or the 4 percent UBT, which carry no sunset. The control table no longer says business rates "run through 2029." The March 2026 one-house support for city corporate-rate authority rests on New York Focus and Spectrum reporting; the resolution texts were not read.
