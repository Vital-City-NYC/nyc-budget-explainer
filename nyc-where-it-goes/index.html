For every $100 the city spends, what does it buy?

One square is $1 of every $100. Click a line to see which agencies spend it, what one of its largest agencies spends money on and who pays for it. The city's fiscal year runs from July 1 to June 30. Figures through fiscal 2025 are audited, meaning what the city actually spent; 2026 and 2027 are budget plans.

2025

    The $118 billion is the audited total for fiscal 2025; the budget adopted in June 2024 was about $112 billion, and taxes and grants came in higher than planned. Pensions, debt service and most health insurance are counted in citywide lines rather than in the services whose workers and buildings run them up; schools are the exception and pay their own staff's health insurance. Counted back the way the Office of Management and Budget itemizes them, police, fire, jails and prosecutors rise from $11 to about $20 of every $100 in fiscal 2025, and schools from $29 to about $32. Shares show each line's slice of the budget, not its size; part three shows dollars per resident.
