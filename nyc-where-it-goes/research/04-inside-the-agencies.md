# 04. Inside the agencies: units of appropriation, fiscal 2027

Compiled 2026-10-02. Source: OMB, *Adopted Budget Fiscal Year 2027, Supporting Schedules*, https://www.nyc.gov/assets/omb/downloads/pdf/adopt26/ss6-26.pdf, "UNIT OF APPROPRIATION SUMMARY" pages, line "APPROPRIATION", adopted column (gross of intra-city sales). Personal-services and other-than-personal-services units with the same name are merged (e.g. Patrol PS + Patrol OTPS). The page shows the six largest units and "everything else." Page ranges per agency (PDF pages): Department Of Education 422-612; Police Department 693-939; Fire Department 966-1064; Admin For Children'S Services 1104-1180; Department Of Social Services 1194-1327; Department Of Homeless Services 1348-1389; Department Of Correction 1398-1462; Housing Preservation And Development 2802-2948; Department Of Health And Mental Hygiene 3025-3278; Department Of Environmental Protect. 3322-3472; Department Of Sanitation 3488-3580; Department Of Transportation 3701-3879; Department Of Parks And Recreation 3889-4046; Department Of Citywide Admin Service 4128-4274.

Department of Education units are grouped editorially: general education instruction and school leadership (401, 402); special education (403, 404, 421, 422, 423, 424, 470 special education pre-K contract payments, 476 due process cases); charter school payments (406); pre-K and early childhood (407-410); fringe benefits for school staff (461); pupil transportation (437, 438); buildings, energy and leases (435, 436, 444); contract schools and foster care payments (472); categorical programs (481, 482); food, safety, technology, support and central offices (415, 416, 433, 434, 439-442, 453, 454, 473-475). Fiscal 2025 audited equivalents are in the Comptroller's ACFR Schedule G5 (PDF pp. 339-378), e.g. p. 344: "406 Charter Schools . . . 3,169,738,673 3,358,499,112 3,358,120,651 378,461" (adopted, modified, actual, better/worse); "438 Pupil Transportation . . . 1,882,656,057 1,918,714,236 1,916,921,192 1,793,044"; "461 Fringe Benefits . . . 4,340,295,426 4,242,684,332 4,226,740,245 15,944,087". The audited schedule's text layout did not parse reliably for every agency, so the page uses the budget schedule and says so.
