## A. What the budget itemizes

Every agency page in OMB27 ends with a note 1 that names the costs held for that agency in three citywide "agencies": the Miscellaneous budget (098, mostly fringe benefits), Pension Contributions (095) and Debt Service (099). Example, Police Department, p. 72E (PDF p. 100):

> "IN ADDITION TO THE 2027 AUTHORIZED BUDGET APPROPRIATION SHOWN ABOVE, ASSOCIATED COSTS FOR FRINGE BENEFITS OF $3,290,629,005 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE MISCELLANEOUS BUDGET, PENSIONS OF $2,643,822,934 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE PENSION CONTRIBUTION AGENCY AND DEBT SERVICE FOR $210,846,123 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE DEBT SERVICE AGENCY."

79 agencies carry such a note; all 79 are listed verbatim in section G. The other agencies on the page (59 community boards, the Districting Commission) have none, so their share stays in the unassigned block.

Totals of the 79 notes: fringe benefits $9,507,509,198; pensions $8,819,414,852; debt service $8,712,630,307.

## C. Schools: what is already in the line and what is not added twice

- Unit 461, OMB27 p. 52E (PDF p. 66): "461 -- FRINGE BENEFITS - PS ... $5,175,073,543" (fiscal 2027 adopted). This is inside the Department of Education's own budget and so already in the schools line, as published.
- Department of Education note 1, p. 54E (PDF p. 68): "IN ADDITION TO THE 2027 AUTHORIZED BUDGET NET TOTAL DEPARTMENT APPROPRIATION SHOWN ABOVE, ASSOCIATED COSTS FOR FRINGE BENEFITS OF $365,955,338 ARE APPROPRIATED IN MISCELLANEOUS BUDGET. THE FRINGE BENEFITS AMOUNT SHOWN INCLUDES INTRA-CITY FUNDS OF $86,459,775 IN THE FY 2027 AUTHORIZED BUDGET FOR WHICH THE APPROPRIATION IS INCLUDED IN THE UNIT OF APPROPRIATION 442. ALSO, ASSOCIATED COSTS FOR DEBT SERVICE OF $2,772,812,000 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE DEBT SERVICE AGENCY WHICH INCLUDES PAYMENTS FOR LEASE PURCHASE AND CITY GUARANTEED DEBT. ADDITIONALLY, ASSOCIATED COSTS FOR PENSIONS OF $2,783,794,768 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE PENSION CONTRIBUTION AGENCY. THE PENSION AMOUNT SHOWN INCLUDES INTRA-CITY FUNDS OF $112,253,972 IN THE FY 2027 AUTHORIZED BUDGET FOR WHICH THE APPROPRIATION IS INCLUDED IN THE UNIT OF APPROPRIATION 482."
- The $86,459,775 and $112,253,972 are paid out of the department's own units 442 and 482, which are already in the schools line, and they match the intra-city sales that 098 and 095 subtract to reach their net totals (section B). So the switch adds $365,955,338 − $86,459,775 = **$279,495,563** of fringe and $2,783,794,768 − $112,253,972 = **$2,671,540,796** of pensions to schools, plus the full $2,772,812,000 of debt service. Unit 461 is not touched.
- With that adjustment the notes account for the pension pool exactly: $8,819,414,852 − $112,253,972 = $8,707,160,880, against a net 095 of $8,707,160,878.

## F. Why the switch shows 2027 only

1. **The audited books have no agency breakdown.** ACFR25 p. 456 carries "Pensions: 095 Pension Contributions" (9,915,575 thousand), "Fringe Benefits and Other Benefit Payments" (8,688,731 thousand) and "Transfers : General Debt Service Fund: 099 Debt Service" (3,801,162 thousand) as citywide lines (research/01, section A). Nothing in the schedule assigns them to agencies.
2. **The only per-agency figures for fiscal 2025 are the plan's, and they do not fit the audited lines.** OMB25's 78 agency notes add to: fringe $8,853,721,608; pensions $10,347,074,733; debt service $7,653,324,115. OMB25 p. 110E (Pension Contributions), fiscal 2025 adopted column: "TOTAL DEPARTMENT ... 10,347,074,734", against $9,915,575 thousand actually paid (ACFR25), so laying the notes over the audited pension line would assign $431 million more than was spent. OMB25 p. 115E (Debt Service): "NET TOTAL DEPARTMENT ... $3,672,112,107" for fiscal 2025. The same page shows "004 -- BUDGET STABILIZATION ACCOUNT ... $4,397,067,648" in the fiscal 2024 modified column, described as "AMOUNTS APPROPRIATED FOR THE PREPAYMENT OF FUTURE YEARS' DEBT SERVICE COSTS." The notes' $7.65 billion of debt service is more than double that year's appropriation, so the per-agency debt figures are not slices of the year's debt line.
3. Any fiscal 2025 view therefore needs a rescaling choice (shares of each pool rather than dollars) and would be an estimate. Fiscal 2026 is also out: OMB27's notes cover only the 2027 appropriation ("IN ADDITION TO THE 2027 AUTHORIZED BUDGET APPROPRIATION"), and the page's 2026 figures are the modified budget.
4. The note under the chart keeps the review's fiscal 2025 estimate, now labeled as one: OMB25's fringe and pension notes added to the audited 2025 lines (no debt service) put police, fire, jails and prosecutors at 19.81 per $100 (published 10.91, 11 squares) and schools at 32.33 (published 28.93, 29 squares); with the Education Department's intra-city amounts taken out (OMB25 p. 55E: "THE FRINGE BENEFITS AMOUNT SHOWN INCLUDES INTRA-CITY FUNDS OF $85,494,638" and "THE PENSION AMOUNT SHOWN INCLUDES INTRA-CITY FUNDS OF $112,253,972"), schools are 32.16. Hence "about $20" and "about $32".

## Check against the review's scratch extraction

The reviewer's `omb_notes.json` (fiscal 2027) matches this extraction for all 79 agencies on fringe and pensions, and on debt service for 78. It misses the Department of Cultural Affairs' debt service because the note runs onto the next page: p. 147E (PDF p. 191) ends "...PENSIONS OF $12,959,141 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE PENSION CONTRIBUTION AGENCY AND DEBT" and PDF p. 192 continues "SERVICE FOR $241,300,723 ARE APPROPRIATED IN THE DEBT SERVICE AGENCY." That $241,300,723 is included here, in housing, transportation, CUNY, parks and libraries. Every note was re-checked so that each cost named in its wording (fringe, pensions, debt service) has a parsed amount.
