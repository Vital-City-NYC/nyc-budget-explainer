# New York City's budget in $100

Four linked visual explainers. Each one is a static page; every number on a page has a URL and a verbatim quotation in that part's `research/` folder.

1. `nyc-who-pays/` Who pays for New York City? Revenue by source, fiscal 2000-2027, with who pays each tax and what Albany controls.
2. `nyc-where-it-goes/` Where the money goes. Spending by line, with agency and unit-of-appropriation splits, who pays for each line, context facts, and what the mayor can change.
3. `nyc-budget-since-2000/` Real dollars per resident by line, fiscal 2000-2027.
4. `nyc-what-the-city-builds/` The capital budget by function, fiscal 2000-2025.

Sources: NYC Comptroller annual comprehensive financial reports; Office of Management and Budget adopted budget documents; Department of Finance; Independent Budget Office; Census Bureau; Bureau of Labor Statistics. Method notes are at the foot of each page.
